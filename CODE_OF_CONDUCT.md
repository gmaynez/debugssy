# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This project exists to build high-quality software. Collaboration should be
professional, technically focused, and respectful.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Treat others with courtesy and professionalism.
- Keep discussions focused on code, design, architecture, and technical
  trade-offs.
- Provide and receive constructive, good-faith feedback.
- Assume competence and good intent unless clearly demonstrated otherwise.
- Use clear, factual, and precise language.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- Personal attacks, name-calling, or derogatory remarks.
- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or invasion of privacy.
- Disruptive behavior, trolling, or bad-faith participation.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Repeated off-topic political, ideological, or non-technical advocacy.
- Any behavior that materially degrades a professional working environment.

Disagreement is expected and encouraged; criticism must target ideas and
implementations, not individuals.

## Enforcement

- Project maintainers are responsible for interpretation and enforcement.
- Responses may include warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent removal
  from the project.
- Enforcement decisions are final and made in the interest of project health.
- Concerns or reports should be communicated privately to maintainers.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces, including repositories, issues, pull
requests, discussions, chats, and events related to the project.

## Philosophy

We value:

- Technical excellence over consensus.
- Clarity over verbosity.
- Outcomes over process.
